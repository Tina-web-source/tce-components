About TCE Components

Practical Sourcing Support for Your Business

TCE Components supports international customers in sourcing electronic components for production, maintenance and project needs. We work with a supplier network to check availability, pricing and lead times across a range of component categories.

Whether you need regularly used components or have a difficult-to-find part on your BOM, send us your part numbers and quantities. We will review the requirements and provide feedback based on current market availability.

  • Support for BOM and multi-line sourcing requests
  • Availability and lead-time checks
  • Communication on part condition and supply options
  • International customer support
